What We Do

One partner from the first enquiry to the goods at your door. We handle every step so you deal with one team, one quote and one point of contact.

Step one

Product Sourcing

We find the right products from vetted manufacturers and distributors, matched to your specification and budget.

Best terms

Supplier Negotiation

We negotiate price, payment terms, lead times and warranties on your behalf, in your interest.

End to end

Procurement

We manage purchase orders, invoices and payments, and keep you updated at every milestone.

Pre-shipment

Quality Assurance

Goods are inspected, counted and photographed before dispatch, so what arrives matches what you ordered.

Logistics & Shipping

What We Handle

Every document, every hand-off

Buying across borders means paperwork and many parties. We take care of it so your order arrives cleared and complete.

  • Supplier vetting and samples
  • Pro forma invoices and purchase orders
  • Pre-shipment inspection with photos
  • Packing lists and labelling
  • Commercial invoices and certificates of origin
  • SADC trade documentation
  • Freight booking and cargo insurance
  • Customs clearance and duties
