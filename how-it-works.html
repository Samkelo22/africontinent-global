Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a quote take?

Most quotes are ready within one business day. Specialised equipment or large orders can take two to three days while we confirm stock and freight.

Is there a minimum order?

No fixed minimum. We handle anything from a single pallet to full container loads, and we can consolidate smaller orders to keep freight costs down.

How do I pay?

You approve a written quote first. Payment terms are agreed per order, usually a deposit on confirmation and the balance before dispatch, by bank transfer.

Do you deliver outside Mozambique?

Yes. We ship across Southern and East Africa, including Botswana, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi and Kenya, by road or sea.

Who handles customs and duties?

We prepare the documents and coordinate clearance with licensed agents. Your quote shows duties and taxes separately so there are no surprises.

Can I see my goods before they ship?

Yes. Every order is inspected before dispatch and we send you photos and a packing list to confirm.

Have a product in mind? Let's source it.

Send us the item, quantity and delivery city. We come back with supplier options and a full landed-cost quote.
