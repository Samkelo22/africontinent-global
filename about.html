About Us

Your Trusted Trade Partner

Africontinent Global is an international procurement and trade agency. We purchase on behalf of clients across Africa and beyond, making sure every order delivers on quality, value and reliability, from the first quote to final delivery.

Why choose us?

Local PresenceTeams on the ground in South Africa and Mozambique.

Client FocusedWe understand your needs and deliver suitable solutions.

ExperienceKnow-how in cross-border trade, customs and regulations.

IntegrityHonest, transparent and professional service.

Our mission

Make cross-border buying simple

To give African businesses and individuals reliable access to quality products, at fair prices, without the risk and paperwork of buying across borders alone.

Our vision

A connected African market

To be the procurement partner that links suppliers and buyers across the continent and beyond, one trusted shipment at a time.
